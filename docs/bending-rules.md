# Avatar bending rules for PvP

This reference uses the animated *Avatar: The Last Airbender* (ATLA) as its baseline. Material from *The Legend of Korra* (Korra) is marked where it expands what Aang's series shows. The last section translates the lore into optional game rules.

## How bending works

Bending is a martial art that lets a person manipulate an element. Stance, movement, breath, focus, and the flow of chi shape how a technique comes out. Large forms are common, but the required movement depends on the technique and the bender's training. Losing the use of your arms can limit your options while leaving footwork, breath, or other trained forms available. A bender usually has one element. The Avatar can learn all four, in the cycle of air → water → earth → fire → air, starting from their native element. Knowing the base art leaves advanced techniques to be learned separately. | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Water | Existing water, including ice and vapor. Skilled benders can extract water from plants or humid air. | Flow between defense and offense, redirect incoming force, then strike or restrain. | Tai chi |

## Waterbending

**Source and conditions.** Waterbenders need water to work with. A waterskin provides a portable supply, while rivers, snow, and ice allow much larger forms. Katara uses her own sweat to escape a cell, and Hama draws water from plants and the air. Early waterbenders learned from the moon's effect on the tides. The full moon strengthens the art. Waterbenders lose their power when Zhao kills the Moon Spirit during the Siege of the North, and regain it after Yue restores the spirit. [Waterbending](https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/Waterbending) · [The Puppetmaster](https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/The_Puppetmaster) · [The Siege of the North, Part 2](https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/The_Siege_of_the_North,_Part_2)

| Style or technique | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Northern Water Tribe | Pakku demonstrates flowing blocks, sweeps, and ice attacks. The North in Aang's time restricted women's formal training to healing until Katara challenged the custom. |
| Southern Water Tribe | Has its own traditions. Hama teaches Katara Southern forms and demonstrates how to find water in unlikely places. |
| Foggy Swamp | Straighter, more rigid motions than polar styles. Huu moves vines by bending the water inside them, forming a plant body or using vines to grab and entangle. |
| Ice and mist | Freezing and melting are part of ordinary waterbending. Ice can form barriers, bindings, or projectiles. Mist can obscure movement. |
| Healing | Uses water to channel chi and treat injuries. It has limits. Katara used water from the Spirit Oasis to revive Aang after Azula struck him with lightning, an exceptional case involving a special source. |
| Bloodbending | Manipulates the water in another living body's tissues to control movement. In ATLA, Hama and Katara use it under a full moon. In Korra, Yakone and his sons are rare exceptions who can use it without a full moon. Amon also uses it to sever a person's access to bending. |
| Spiritbending (Korra) | A specialized waterbending technique that can calm or corrupt spirits. |

## Earthbending

**Source and conditions.** Earthbenders move available material, including stone and loose sand. Badgermoles inspired the art. A planted stance supports traditional forms, and being cut off from bendable material can leave a bender unable to use them. Feet touching the ground matter especially for seismic sense. Earthbending itself can also be performed with other movements. [Earthbending](https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/Earthbending) · [Bitter Work](https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/Bitter_Work) · [Imprisoned](https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/Imprisoned)

| Style or technique | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Traditional earthbending | Raises walls and pillars, launches stone, and reshapes terrain. A raised slab can block a strike and then become a projectile. |
| Sandbending | Desert specialists move loose sand into waves or clouds and use it to propel sand-sailers. Loose sand also makes Toph's early seismic reads less clear. |
| Dai Li | Use small, precise forms, including launched stone gloves that grab or pin opponents and stone shoes for moving on walls and ceilings. |
| Seismic sense | Reads vibrations through connected ground. Toph uses it to track movement and recognize shapes. An airborne opponent gives her little ground vibration until contact resumes. |
| Metalbending | Toph discovers it late in ATLA by sensing and moving residual earth inside refined metal. Korra establishes highly purified platinum as beyond metalbenders' reach. By that era, metal cables, armor, and police gear make the technique a developed fighting style. |
| Lavabending | Controls molten earth and can turn solid earth into lava or back again. ATLA shows Avatar Roku manipulating lava. Korra shows how specialists Ghazan and Bolin use it in combat, including lava discs and changes to the battlefield. |

## Firebending

**Source and conditions.** Breath and internal energy let firebenders generate flames without carrying fuel. They can also control fire already present. The sun strengthens their bending, a total solar eclipse temporarily removes it, and Sozin's Comet dramatically amplifies it. Firebenders can still bend at night. In ordinary combat, fire can escape the bender's control and spread, as when Zhao burns his own ships. [Firebending](https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/Firebending) · [The Day of Black Sun, Part 2](https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/The_Day_of_Black_Sun,_Part_2:_The_Eclipse) · [The Deserter](https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/The_Deserter)

| Style or technique | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Fire Nation military forms | Fast, direct strikes and kicks send flame forward in bursts or arcs. The wartime tradition often teaches anger and aggression as fuel. |
| Sun Warrior forms | The dragons teach Aang and Zuko to understand fire as life and energy. The Dancing Dragon is a set of coordinated forms that illustrates this tradition. |
| Breath, jets, and blue flame | Fire breath and jet propulsion extend what fire can do. Azula's blue flame is a distinctive feat. Its color alone supplies no universal combat multiplier. |
| Lightning generation | Iroh teaches that a skilled firebender separates two energies and guides their release as lightning. His lesson emphasizes calm and precision. ATLA portrays it as rare. Korra shows faster releases and industrial use. |
| Lightning redirection | A separate skill from generation. Iroh adapts waterbending principles to guide incoming lightning through the body and out the other arm, keeping its path away from the heart. Zuko learns to redirect without mastering generation in ATLA. |
| Combustionbending | Combustion Man focuses chi through the forehead for a ranged explosion. A hit to that point can disrupt the shot and cause it to backfire. Korra's P'Li can curve her shots. |

## Airbending

**Source and conditions.** Air is available in most arenas. Air Nomads learned from flying bison and favor mobility and evasion. Their approach gives airbenders ways to avoid a direct clash, push opponents away, deflect projectiles, or use their own motion to build a counterattack. [Airbending](https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/Airbending) · [Flying bison](https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_bison)

| Style or technique | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Air Nomad forms | Circling, sweeping motions, and quick changes of direction. Gusts, air swipes, and vortices can knock enemies back or disrupt their attacks. |
| Staff gliding and air scooter | Aang uses a folding glider with air currents for aerial travel and rides a moving ball of air for rapid repositioning. These are separate techniques from unassisted flight. |
| Unassisted flight (Korra) | Guru Laghima and Zaheer achieve true flight through release of earthly attachments. It is exceptionally rare. |
| Spiritual projection (Korra) | An airbender with a strong spiritual connection, such as Jinora, can send an intangible projection away from their body. |
| Removing breathable air (Korra) | Zaheer demonstrates that airbending can suffocate. The show depicts it as a deliberate, dangerous use of ordinary control over air. |

## The Avatar and chi-blocking

- **Avatar State.** A heightened state that lets the Avatar draw on the skill and power of past lives during Aang's era. Aang can use the elements together at a scale far beyond ordinary fighters. If an Avatar dies in the Avatar State, the reincarnation cycle ends. [Avatar State](https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/Avatar_State)
- **Energybending.** An ancient art concerned with the energy within a person. A lion turtle teaches Aang, who uses it to remove Ozai's firebending. Its use in the finale involves a contest of spirit. [Energybending](https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/Energybending)
- **Chi-blocking.** Ty Lee uses precise physical strikes to interrupt movement and temporarily suppress bending. A trained nonbender can use it. [Chi-blocking](https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/Chi-blocking)

## Optional PvP rules

The rules below are design proposals. The series gives you material constraints and examples of techniques interacting. Timing windows, damage, range, and resource budgets are game decisions.

1. **Make the arena matter.** Mark bendable stone, sand, water, and bendable metal clearly. Give water players a small starting supply and places to recover it. Put enough stone within reach for earth players to open a fight. Let larger moves draw on visible reserves so the map changes how each element plays without deciding the matchup at spawn.
2. **Build each base kit around a different response.** Water catches a committed attack and turns it into a whip or ice bind. Earth creates cover and punishes a committed approach. Fire makes opponents answer successive attacks at close to mid range. Air changes angles, interrupts charges, and knocks opponents off good positions.
3. **Resolve the move, material, and angle.** A stone wall can intercept a fire blast. A water shield can extinguish a manageable flame. A gust can deflect a loose projectile or fan an existing fire. Lightning redirection can answer a bolt when the defender is trained, free to move, and facing the attack. Give each interaction a readable animation and an opening on failure.
4. **Make rare techniques distinct loadouts.** A metalbender can trade some terrain control for cable grabs and movement. A combustionbender needs a visible wind-up and a vulnerable forehead. Give lightning generation and redirection separate moves. Seismic sense can reveal grounded motion through connected terrain and drop the read when a target jumps. Each specialist still uses the basics of their own element.
5. **Use the era to set eligibility.** For an Aang-era roster, use the four base arts, ATLA regional styles, ATLA rare techniques, and chi-blocking. Treat metalbending as Toph's late-series discovery and ordinary lava specialists as a later option. For a Korra-era roster, add established metalbending schools, non-Avatar lavabenders, spiritbending, and the later air techniques as appropriate.
6. **Reserve overwhelming powers for modes built around them.** Put Avatar State, Sozin's Comet, full-moon bloodbending, and eclipse events in scenarios with clear objectives and telegraphed timing. In a mode featuring bloodbending, a brief interruptible hold preserves the fantasy and gives the target a path back into play. For an Avatar encounter, several ordinary fighters can contest objectives while the Avatar has the stronger kit.
